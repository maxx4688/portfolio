Overview

GreenLeaf is a brand identity project for a fictional eco-friendly startup. The design system uses organic shapes, earth-tone greens, and clean typography to convey trust, sustainability, and modern craftsmanship.

Deliverables include a primary logo, secondary marks, color specifications, business card layouts, and social media templates — all built for consistent cross-platform use.
