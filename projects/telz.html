Overview

Telz is a clean, minimal messenger built with Flutter. Pick a unique username, find people, and start talking, in real time, with notifications that actually reach you.

Conversations sync instantly through Firebase Realtime Database and are cached on the device, so chats open quickly. Cloud Messaging delivers new messages even when the app is closed.

🚧 Beta testing is under progress, and feedback is very welcome.

Built With

  • Flutter for a single, fast cross-platform codebase
  • Firebase Auth for secure sign-up and sign-in
  • Realtime Database for messages that sync instantly
  • Cloud Messaging for push notifications
  • Riverpod for state management
  • Supabase for FCM Admin side deployment and profile picture storage

Features

Everything you need to start a conversation, and nothing that gets in the way.

💬

Real-time chat

  • One-to-one conversations that sync instantly
  • Home screen with all your conversations, latest message preview and time
  • Unread badges so you never miss who is waiting on you
  • Typing indicator, so you know when the other person is replying
🔍

Find people

  • Search for people by their username
  • Jump straight from a profile into a conversation
🔔

Push notifications

  • Get notified about new messages even when the app is closed
  • Turn notifications on or off whenever you like
🙋

Your profile

  • Choose a unique @username with live availability checking while you type
  • Edit your profile any time
  • Add and manage your profile picture
  • Copy your email and username with a tap
  • Change your password with an emailed reset link
🎨

Made to feel yours

  • Light and dark themes
  • Adjustable message text size
  • Optional haptic feedback
  • Typing indicator toggle for more privacy
⚡

Fast and reliable

  • Messages and conversations are cached on your device, so chats open quickly
  • Secure sign-up and sign-in
